SHARE LAB / RESEARCH

Research

Uncertainty quantification for understanding system–hazard interactions and advancing infrastructure resilience.

01 / RESEARCH AREA

Uncertainty Quantification

Quantifying uncertainties in hazards, structural properties, numerical models, and system performance.

  • Probabilistic modeling and uncertainty propagation
  • Structural reliability and risk assessment
  • Surrogate modeling and data-driven simulation
02 / RESEARCH AREA

Natural Hazards

Characterizing natural hazards and their interactions with buildings and infrastructure systems.

  • Extreme weather and multi-hazard processes
  • Hazard characterization and event analysis
  • Compound and cascading disaster scenarios
03 / RESEARCH AREA

Wind Engineering

Understanding extreme wind environments and wind-induced structural performance.

  • Downburst and non-synoptic winds
  • Wind loading and dynamic structural response
  • Wind hazard prediction and assessment
04 / RESEARCH AREA

Structural Engineering

Evaluating the behavior and safety of structural systems using mechanics and computational analysis.

  • Bridge and cable-supported structures
  • Nonlinear structural analysis
  • Performance and safety assessment
05 / RESEARCH AREA

Infrastructure Resilience

Assessing risk and resilience across interconnected infrastructure systems exposed to natural hazards.

  • System-level risk and vulnerability
  • Resilience-based performance assessment
  • Risk-informed decision support
